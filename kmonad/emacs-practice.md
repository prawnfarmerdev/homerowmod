# Emacs Practice with Homerow Mods

Practice these Emacs shortcuts using the homerow mod configuration.

## Basic Navigation

- `C-f` (forward char) – hold D (left Ctrl), tap F
- `C-b` (backward char) – hold D, tap B
- `C-n` (next line) – hold D, tap N
- `C-p` (previous line) – hold D, tap P
- `C-a` (beginning of line) – hold D, tap A
- `C-e` (end of line) – hold D, tap E

## Word Navigation

- `M-f` (forward word) – hold S (left Alt), tap F
- `M-b` (backward word) – hold S, tap B

## Editing

- `C-d` (delete char) – hold D, tap D
- `M-d` (delete word) – hold S, tap D
- `C-k` (kill line) – hold D, tap K
- `C-y` (yank) – hold D, tap Y
- `C-/` or `C-_` (undo) – hold D, tap / or _
- `C-x u` (undo) – hold D, tap X, then tap U

## Files and Buffers

- `C-x C-f` (find file) – hold D, tap X, tap F
- `C-x C-s` (save file) – hold D, tap X, tap S
- `C-x C-w` (save as) – hold D, tap X, tap W
- `C-x b` (switch buffer) – hold D, tap X, tap B
- `C-x k` (kill buffer) – hold D, tap X, tap K

## Windows and Frames

- `C-x 2` (split window vertically) – hold D, tap X, tap 2
- `C-x 3` (split window horizontally) – hold D, tap X, tap 3
- `C-x o` (other window) – hold D, tap X, tap O
- `C-x 0` (delete window) – hold D, tap X, tap 0
- `C-x 1` (delete other windows) – hold D, tap X, tap 1

## Searching

- `C-s` (search forward) – hold D, tap S
- `C-r` (search backward) – hold D, tap R
- `M-%` (query replace) – hold S, tap %

## Help

- `C-h t` (tutorial) – hold D, tap H, tap T
- `C-h k` (describe key) – hold D, tap H, tap K
- `C-h f` (describe function) – hold D, tap H, tap F

## Practice Exercises

1. Open a new file: `C-x C-f practice.txt RET`
2. Type a sentence, then navigate with `C-f`, `C-b`, `C-n`, `C-p`.
3. Move by words with `M-f`, `M-b`.
4. Delete characters with `C-d`, words with `M-d`.
5. Kill a line with `C-k`, yank it back with `C-y`.
6. Undo with `C-/`.
7. Save the file with `C-x C-s`.
8. Split window with `C-x 2`, switch with `C-x o`, close with `C-x 0`.
9. Search for a word with `C-s`.
10. Open the tutorial with `C-h t`.

## Tips

- Use the opposite hand for modifiers when possible (e.g., hold left Ctrl while tapping right hand keys).
- For combined modifiers like `C-M-f`, hold D (Ctrl) and S (Alt) together, then tap F.
- If a modifier activates accidentally (you get a capital letter or unexpected behavior), try tapping more quickly.
- If a modifier doesn't activate, hold the key a bit longer.

Remember: practice builds muscle memory. Start slowly, then increase speed.
